# How to Add Memo in Phantom Wallet

## ⚠️ CRITICAL: The memo field is REQUIRED for payment verification!

Without the memo, your payment **cannot be automatically detected** and your balance will not be updated.

---

## 📱 Mobile App Instructions (iOS/Android)

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Open Phantom Wallet App**
   - Launch the app on your phone

2. **Tap "Send" Button**
   - Usually at the bottom of the screen

3. **Select Token Type**
   - Choose USDC or USDT (whichever your payment requires)

4. **Enter Recipient Address**
   - Paste the wallet address from your payment instructions
   - Example: `8qhgYTV4wJFsEEwiZqTwzuJisFrimUXQG9MLsHGkkWU8`

5. **Enter Amount**
   - Type the exact amount shown (e.g., 10.00)

6. **⚠️ IMPORTANT: Add Memo**
   
   **Option A: Look for "Add Memo" button**
   - Scroll down on the send screen
   - Tap **"Add Memo"** or **"Advanced Options"**
   - A text field will appear
   
   **Option B: Look for settings icon**
   - Look for a **⚙️ gear icon** or **three dots ⋮** on the send screen
   - Tap it to reveal advanced options
   - Enable **"Add Memo"** toggle
   - A text field will appear

7. **Paste the Memo**
   - Long-press in the memo field
   - Select **"Paste"**
   - Verify the entire memo is copied exactly
   - Example: `Username: brand123 Amount: 50 ReferenceID: AB12CD34`

8. **Review Everything**
   - ✅ Correct wallet address
   - ✅ Correct amount (exact)
   - ✅ Memo is present and correct
   - ✅ Correct token (USDC or USDT)

9. **Confirm & Send**
   - Tap **"Send"** or **"Confirm"**
   - Approve the transaction with Face ID/PIN

10. **Save Transaction Signature** (Optional)
    - After sending, you'll see a transaction signature
    - You can copy this for manual verification if needed

## 💻 Browser Extension Instructions (Chrome/Firefox/Brave)

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Click Phantom Extension Icon**
   - In your browser toolbar

2. **Click "Send" Button**

3. **Fill in Details**
   - **To**: Paste wallet address
   - **Amount**: Enter exact amount
   - **Token**: Select USDC or USDT

4. **Scroll Down to Find Memo Field**
   - The memo field is **below the amount field**
   - Look for **"Memo (optional)"** text
   - It may be collapsed/hidden

5. **Click to Expand Memo Section**
   - Click on **"Memo (optional)"** to expand it
   - A text input field will appear

6. **Paste Your Memo**
   - Click in the memo field
   - Paste your memo exactly as shown
   - Example: `Username: brand123 Amount: 50 ReferenceID: AB12CD34`

7. **Review & Send**
   - Double-check all details
   - Click **"Next"** or **"Continue"**
   - Click **"Approve"** to confirm transaction

8. **Transaction Complete**
   - You'll see a success message
   - Transaction signature will be displayed
   - Your payment will be processed within 1-2 minutes

## 🔍 Where is the Memo Field?

### Common Locations:

**Mobile App:**
- Scroll down after entering amount
- Under "Advanced" or "Options" section
- May be labeled as "Add Memo", "Memo", or "Add Note"
- Sometimes behind a ⚙️ settings icon

**Browser Extension:**
- Below the amount input field
- Section labeled "Memo (optional)"
- Click to expand if collapsed
- Sometimes in "Advanced Options"

## ⚠️ Troubleshooting

### "I still don't see a memo field"

**Try these:**

1. **Update Phantom Wallet**
   - Go to app store / extension store
   - Check for updates
   - Install latest version

2. **Look for "Advanced" Options**
   - Tap/click "Show Advanced Options"
   - Tap/click "More Options"
   - Look for ⚙️ gear icon or ⋮ three dots

3. **Different Send Screen Layouts**
   - Some versions have memo on first screen
   - Some show it after clicking "Next"
   - Some hide it in advanced options

4. **Desktop vs Mobile**
   - Desktop extension: Usually visible by default
   - Mobile app: Often needs to be manually enabled

### "I sent payment without memo"

**Don't worry!** You can still manually verify:

1. **Get your transaction signature**
   - In Phantom, go to transaction history
   - Find the transaction you just sent
   - Copy the transaction signature (long string of characters)

2. **Manual Verification**
   - Go back to the payment screen in our app
   - Click "I've Sent the Payment"
   - Enter your transaction signature
   - Click "Verify Payment Now"

3. **Contact Support**
   - If manual verification fails, contact support
   - Provide: transaction signature, reference ID, and amount
   - We can manually verify and credit your account

## ✅ Verification Checklist

Before clicking "Send", verify:

- [ ] Wallet address is correct (matches exactly)
- [ ] Amount is exact (e.g., 50.00 not 50)
- [ ] Memo is present in the memo field
- [ ] Memo is exactly as shown (case-sensitive)
- [ ] Correct token selected (USDC or USDT)
- [ ] Correct network (Devnet for testing, Mainnet for production)

---

## 🎯 Quick Tips

1. **Always copy-paste the memo** - Don't type it manually
2. **Check for trailing spaces** - Memo must be exact
3. **Case-sensitive** - "Username" is not same as "username"
4. **Include everything** - The entire memo string
5. **Don't modify** - Don't add or remove any characters
6. **Update Phantom** - Use latest version for best experience
