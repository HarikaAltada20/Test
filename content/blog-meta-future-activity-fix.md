### HTML Content

```html
<p>If you're trying to connect your Instagram account or submit content to a contest and see this error:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p><strong>OAuthException:</strong> Your account's future activity history off Meta technologies is currently turned off. Please visit account center to change it.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>Don't worry—this is a common Meta privacy setting, and you can fix it in a few minutes. This guide walks you through the solution step by step.</p>

<h2>What Is "Future Activity History"?</h2>

<p>Meta (Facebook and Instagram) lets you control whether apps and websites can track your activity across their products. When "future activity" is turned <strong>off</strong>, Instagram-connected apps can't access your content or insights, which blocks you from:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Connecting your Instagram account to Game Of Creators</li>
  <li>Loading your Reels for contest submissions</li>
  <li>Fetching views and engagement data</li>
</ul>

<p>Turning it <strong>on</strong> only allows the apps you choose to access this data. You stay in control.</p>

<h2>Step-by-Step: Enable Future Activity in Meta Account Center</h2>

<h3>Step 1: Open Instagram Settings</h3>

<p>On the Instagram app or <a href="https://www.instagram.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Instagram.com</a>, tap your profile icon, then:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>App:</strong> Tap the menu (☰) → <strong>Settings</strong></li>
  <li><strong>Website:</strong> Click your profile picture → <strong>Settings</strong></li>
</ul>

<h3>Step 2: Go to Accounts Center</h3>

<p>Scroll down and tap <strong>"See more in Accounts Center"</strong> (or "Account Center").</p>

<h3>Step 3: Open Activity Settings</h3>

<p>In Accounts Center, go to:</p>
<p><strong>Your information and permissions</strong> → <strong>Your activity off Meta technologies</strong></p>

<h3>Step 4: Manage Future Activity</h3>

<p>Tap <strong>"Manage future activity"</strong>.</p>

<h3>Step 5: Turn On Connect Future Activity</h3>

<p>Find the option <strong>"Connect future activity"</strong> and turn it <strong>ON</strong>. Confirm when asked.</p>

<h3>Step 6: Try Again</h3>

<p>Return to Game Of Creators and try connecting your Instagram account or submitting your Reel again. The error should be resolved.</p>

<h2>Direct Link: Meta Account Center</h2>

<p>You can also open the activity settings directly:</p>
<p><a href="https://www.facebook.com/accountcenter/activity" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Open Meta Account Center – Your Activity</a></p>
<p>From there, follow <strong>Manage future activity</strong> → <strong>Connect future activity</strong> → turn it ON.</p>

<h2>Still Having Trouble?</h2>

<p>If you've enabled future activity and still see the error:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Wait a few minutes and try again—settings can take a moment to apply</li>
  <li>Log out of Instagram and log back in</li>
  <li>If connecting from the app, try connecting from a web browser instead (or vice versa)</li>
</ul>

<p>For more help, contact our support team or visit your <a href="/dashboard/settings">Settings</a> page to re-connect your Instagram account.</p>
```

**Intro**
If you see "Your account's future activity history off Meta technologies is currently turned off," this guide shows you how to fix it so you can connect Instagram and submit to opportunities.

**What is Future Activity?**
It's a Meta privacy setting. When it's off, apps can't access your Instagram data. Turning it on lets chosen apps (like Game Of Creators) access your content and insights.

**Steps:**
1. Open Instagram → Settings
2. Tap "See more in Accounts Center"
3. Go to "Your information and permissions" → "Your activity off Meta technologies"
4. Tap "Manage future activity"
5. Turn ON "Connect future activity"
6. Try connecting or submitting again

**Direct link:** https://www.facebook.com/accountcenter/activity

**Troubleshooting:** Wait a few minutes, log out and back in, or try a different browser/device.
